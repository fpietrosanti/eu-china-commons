Open Source Software Country Intelligence

OSOR’s open source software country intelligence provides insights into open source policies, legal frameworks, actors and initiatives across EU Member States and countries beyond the EU. On this page, you will find:

  • Country intelligence reports and factsheets: Country reports provide an overview of relevant policy and legal frameworks, key actors and selected open source initiatives. Factsheets visually summarise the reports. The reports are informed by desk research and, where possible, input from national experts and contact points.
  • Progress and trends reports: OSOR also publishes regular analytical reports that draw on country intelligence to identify broader developments in open source policy, governance, legal frameworks, reuse and public-sector adoption. These reports highlight progress, common challenges and emerging trends.

If you are interested in following our activities in this domain more closely or providing us with feedback, please do not hesitate to email us at EU-OSOR@ec.europa.eu.
