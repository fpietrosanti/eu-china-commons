TOOLS | Decidim: open source software developed by Barcelona City Council to facilitate participatory democracy

12 July 2019
Resources Governance and Partnerships Residents and Talents

SUMMARY

Decidim is a citizen participation platform that enables local residents to take part in the development of public policies: taking part in debates and consultations, responding to a referendum, proposing new ideas, voting for investments using participatory budgets, etc. This open source software has already been adopted by around a hundred local authorities and private players worldwide.

Keywords: participatory budget, civic tech, community of practice, participatory democracy, citizen engagement, innovation, free software, open source, citizen participation, platform, Barcelona, Spain
