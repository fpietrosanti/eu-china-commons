[Offline Half-day Training] Workforce Optimization in the Current Environment: Pain Points and Practical Solutions 新形势下企业人员优化的痛点难点与破解之道

Companies today are caught between two pressures at once — adjusting their workforce and getting departing employees back into work — and this has made workforce optimization harder in every scenario. Drawing on its front-line experience in human resources and employment law, River Delta Law Firm has distilled the pain points companies are facing and the practical ways through them. The course focuses on the collective and individual workforce optimization issues companies are most concerned about right now. Drawing on case experience from legal practice, the speaker will share typical cases and explore where the protection of employees' lawful rights ends and an employer's discretion over its workforce begins — with the aim of helping companies manage employment relations as effectively as possible. 当前形势下，企业用工调整与员工再就业的双重压力叠加，给企业各类情形下的人员优化带来了新的挑战。江三角律师事务所凭借在人力资源和劳动用工法律服务领域深耕一线的经验，特别总结梳理了企业于当前进行人员优化的痛点难点与破解之道。 本次课程将根据企业的实际需求，对其当下最为关心的涵盖群体与个体员工优化问题，与大家分享基于律师实务的经验与典型案例，共同探讨新形势下劳动者合法权益保护与企业人力资源管理的边界，以期最大限度帮助企业提升劳动关系管理效能。

  • 北京长富宫饭店二层茉莉厅

Cheers to Christmas 2026 in Chengdu 2026成都圣诞社交酒会

The European Chamber Southwest China Chapter and the Italian Chamber of Commerce in China (CCIC) are delighted to invite you to our joint year-end celebration: our Christmas Initiative - Cheers to Christmas 2026 in Chengdu, taking place on Thursday, November 26th, 2026 at Shi Jing of Tonino Lamborghini Hotel Chengdu. 中国欧盟商会西南分会与中国意大利商会（CCIC）诚挚邀请您参加我们联合举办的2026成都圣诞社交酒会，活动将于 2026 年 11 月 26 日（周四）在成都托尼洛·兰博基尼酒店时境厅举办。 As the year draws to a close and the Christmas season approaches, two of Southwest China’s most influential international business communities come together for this festive evening. Beijing > Other

Breakfast Roundtable with Dr. WANG Dan | How Is Geopolitical Change Shaping China’s Economic Transition?

China’s growth model is evolving amid shifts in US-China trade and technology relations, China-EU economic ties, global trade policy and energy markets. Exports and advanced manufacturing remain important sources of growth, while subdued domestic demand and structural challenges make rebalancing more difficult. Against this backdrop, the European Chamber invites you to a breakfast roundtable in Beijing on Friday, 27 November, featuring Dr. WANG Dan, China Director at Eurasia Group. Drawing on her expertise in China’s macroeconomy and geopolitics, Dr. WANG Dan will discuss how external developments and domestic challenges are shaping China’s economic outlook and policy priorities.

  • 08:00 - 10:00
  • Workshop 1, 3rd Floor, EAST Beijing 北京东隅酒店三层会议室1
