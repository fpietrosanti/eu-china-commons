COSCon'26 | Call for Topics: AI-Native Open Source Operating System Forum

2026-10-22 09:59:03

As one of the most influential open-source events in the industry, the China Open Source Conference COSCon'26 will be held on November 14-15 at the Hangzhou Cloud Valley Center. With the theme “Next Generation of Open Source,” this conference directly addresses the technological paradigm shift brought by AI, gathering forces from multiple sides to explore the development paths for next-generation open-source technology, talent, community, and business.


Bythe OpenAtom openKylin (abbreviated as “openKylin”) communityproduced, the“AI-Native Open Source Operating System Forum”call for proposals has been launched. The forum focuses on the all-scenario innovation of open-source operating systems under the AI wave, soliciting topics from industry-wide technical experts, open-source contributors, university researchers, and developers, to jointly explore the path of technological innovation and industrial implementation of open-source operating systems in the AI wave.


Call for proposals themes for this forum


  • Core underlying technologies of AI-native operating systems
  • Intelligent Implementation Practice of Open-Source Operating Systems
  • Software-Hardware Collaboration and Full-Scenario Ecosystem Co-Construction
  • Community Governance, Talent Development, and Industry Trend Reflections

From now until October 31, openKylin sincerely invites you to submit a topic sharing for this forum. Scan the QR code below, select in the 【Topic】 dropdown  “AI-Native Open-Source Operating System Forum”  to submit your application.


Important Schedule


  • Call for topics deadline: October 31, 2026
  • Full agenda announced: November 6, 2026

Topic Review Process


The openKylin community will organize a review committee to evaluate all submitted topics, and the review results will be communicated before November 6. Selected topics will be presented live at the "AI Native Open Source Operating System Forum"; high-quality topics that are not selected will be included in the community's technical discussion pool for ongoing technical exchanges.

We sincerely invite partners deeply engaged in the fields of foundational software, AI, and open source to actively submit topics, share technical practices and cutting-edge insights on the stage of COSCon'26, and join hands to build an open-source operating system ecosystem in the AI era.
