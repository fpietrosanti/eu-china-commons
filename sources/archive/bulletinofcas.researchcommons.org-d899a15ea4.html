Development Experience of International Open Source and Its Enlightenment to Construction of Open Source Innovation System in China

Authors

  • Yuntao LONG, Institutes of Science and Development, Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing 100190, China School of Public Policy and Management, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing 100049, ChinaFollow
  • Xiaoming WANG, Institutes of Science and Development, Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing 100190, China School of Public Policy and Management, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing 100049, China
  • Rong GU, State Key Laboratory for Novel Software Technology, Nanjing University, Nanjing 210023, China Department of Computer Science and Technology, Nanjing University, Nanjing 210023, China
  • Yungang BAO, Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing 100190, ChinaFollow

Keywords

open source foundation; open source governance; open source ecosystem; open source; innovation system

Abstract

Open source has become an essential innovation model for global technological progress and the construction of an open source innovation system is an important path for China to achieve self-reliance and self-improvement in science and technology. As an indispensable part of the global software value chain and high-tech industry chain, China needs to improve its open source innovation ecosystem. The paper, based on a systematic review of the related development policies and successful experiences on the open source work in US and Europe, puts forward a series of policy suggestions to solve the practical problems faced by the construction of open source ecosystem and promote the construction of an open source innovation system in China.
