# Brand and .eu domain — options (for decision)

DNS check on 2 Nov 2026: NXDOMAIN means *probably* free. **Confirm at an EURid registrar** before deciding. A .eu domain needs a registrant resident in the EU/EEA.

## Naming criteria
- Easy to read and say for a European **and** for a Chinese speaker; pinyin works for both.
- Positive in Chinese and free of awkward homophones.
- Avoid politically loaded terms: 丝路 (Belt and Road), 天下 (*tianxia*), "sovereignty" in the name itself.
- In Chinese the usual order is 中欧 (China–Europe). Use it in the ZH name; keep "European and Chinese" in EN.

## Shortlist

| Domain | Meaning / resonance | For a European | For a Chinese reader | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **gongxiang.eu** | 共享 *gòngxiǎng* = "to share". Also part of the official formula 共建共享 ("build together, share together") | Neutral, short, exotic but readable | **Very strong**: familiar, positive, cooperative | Recommended as the bridge domain. NXDOMAIN |
| **commons-bridge.eu** | "bridge between the commons" | Very clear | Translates naturally as 公地之桥 | NXDOMAIN; the clearest choice for EU institutions |
| **opencommons.eu** | open commons | Excellent, generic | Clear (开放公地) | NXDOMAIN; very generic, risk of confusion with other projects |
| kaiyuan-commons.eu | 开源 *kāiyuán* = open source | Opaque | Excellent | kaiyuan.eu is already taken; overlaps with KAIYUANSHE (开源社) |
| gongjian.eu | 共建 = "build together" | Opaque | Positive, but strongly tied to official 一带一路共建 language | Not recommended: political connotation |
| eu-china-commons.eu | literal | Clear | Clear | NXDOMAIN; puts the countries before the commons, which goes against the "global" framing |

**Avoid:** *gongdi* (公地, commons) is a homophone of 工地 "construction site". *hehe* (和合, harmony) reads in internet slang as 呵呵, a sarcastic laugh.

## Recommendation
- **Main domain `commons-bridge.eu`**: institutionally clear in Europe.
- **Alias `gongxiang.eu`**: redirects to the ZH version, for Chinese audiences.
- Name: EN *Observatory on European and Chinese Digital Commons*, ZH **中欧数字公地观察站**, short brand **Commons Bridge · 共享桥**. "共享桥" is a proposal; it needs native review.
- Total cost: 2 × .eu ≈ €10–20/year.
