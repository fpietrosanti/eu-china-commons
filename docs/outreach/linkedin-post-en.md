🇪🇺🤝🇨🇳 Launching a new research project: the Europe–China Digital Commons Observatory (中欧数字公地观察站)

Open-source software, open standards and open data are becoming the public infrastructure of the digital economy. Europe and China both invest heavily in them, through public funding, industrial policy and open-source foundations, yet each side knows surprisingly little about what the other is doing.

The Observatory aims to:
🔹 map open-source projects funded with public money in the EU, its member states, China and its provinces;
🔹 study open-source industrial policy and its economic impact;
🔹 build a directory of research bodies, foundations, companies and cooperation mechanisms between Europe and China;
🔹 support evidence-based dialogue and cooperation on the digital commons.

We are at an early research stage, and all data and methods are open on GitHub.

📣 Call for collaboration: if you are a researcher, open-source community member, foundation or industry practitioner, or policy expert interested in EU–China digital cooperation, get in touch. We are looking for people to help with data collection, review and joint research.
