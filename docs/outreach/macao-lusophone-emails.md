## 1. FCT — International Relations (priority 1, inquiry S1)
**To:** FCT International Relations (via https://www.fct.pt/en/sobre/contactos/)
**Subject:** Inquiry: FCT–FDCT cooperation and digital commons research

Dear Colleagues,

I am writing on behalf of the Europe–China Digital Commons Observatory (https://eu-china-commons.eu), an independent, open research project mapping how Europe and China fund, govern and reuse open-source software, open standards and open data.

We are aware of the 2017 memorandum between FCT and Macao's Science and Technology Development Fund (FDCT) and of the 2019 joint call. Our research agenda (https://eu-china-commons.eu/roadmap/) includes cross-border funding for collaboration on digital public goods, and the Portugal–Macao channel is a specific case we would like to document correctly.

Could you tell us whether further FCT–FDCT joint calls are planned, and whether digital commons or open-source topics could be eligible? We would also be glad to include FCT's perspective in our work.

Kind regards,
Fabio Pietrosanti
Europe–China Digital Commons Observatory · contact@eu-china-commons.eu
