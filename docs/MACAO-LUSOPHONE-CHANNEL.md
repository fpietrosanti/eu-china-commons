Companion to [COLLAB-MAP.md](COLLAB-MAP.md) (roadmap track G). Records: [../data/stakeholders/collab-map.json](../data/stakeholders/collab-map.json) (ids `mo-*`, `gba-hengqin`, `pt-*`, `eu-eeas-hkmo`, plus the existing `forum-macao`, `pt-ipor`, `pt-aicep`, `pt-ccilc`, `pt-fct`, `pt-esop`). Source rows: `tools/collab_additions_macao.js`. Research date: 2026-11-10.

**Status: analyst working paper. Scenarios are proposals or inferences, labelled as such. Nothing here has been discussed with any organisation; no outreach has taken place.**

Scope rationale: Macao is included as part of the Greater Bay Area and for its established role as a platform between China and Portuguese-speaking countries.

## (a) Executive summary

- The map now holds 17 new records for this channel (12 in Macao and the Hengqin zone, 4 in Portugal, 1 EU office) on top of 6 existing Macao/Portugal records. Sixteen are evidence A (official homepage fetched and identity read); the Portuguese consulate is D (the site returned HTTP 503 on the check date).
- The most concrete finding is a **funding route**: Macao's FDCT has a 2017 memorandum with Portugal's FCT (one joint call in 2019 on marine themes, EUR 300,000 in total, reported) and joint programmes with mainland agencies (NSFC-FDCT 2026 round; FDCT-MOST and Guangdong calls in 2024). We found **no confirmed FCT-FDCT call after 2019** and the FCT page was last updated December 2022. Whether a digital-commons topic could be put on a future call is an open question, not a finding.
- The second finding is a **language capability**: Macao Polytechnic University reports a Chinese-Portuguese machine translation and AI centre; the Forum Macao and IPIM both operate in Portuguese, Chinese and English. This fits track C (translation, community) and E (interoperability).
- Portuguese-language specificity creates a modest **notification duty of courtesy**, not a legal one: if we publish Portuguese-language material or name Portuguese institutions, the bodies in section (c) should hear it from us first.
- The channel is a dissemination and funding-route option, not a source of open-source policy evidence. None of the new records has a documented open-source remit (stated per record in `likelihood_reason`).

## (d) Risks and caveats

- **Evidence.** Evidence A means the official homepage was read and the organisation exists as described; it does not validate any value proposition or likelihood, all of which are inferences. Press and search-summary items (FDCT calls, MPU centre, One Account) are not verified on the issuing body's own pages.
- **Stale or moved pages.** FCT's bilateral page dates from 2022; older FCT pages exist only in archives; FDCT's former domain shows a change notice. Calls and amounts must be re-checked at source before any decision.
- **Funding fit.** Joint schemes fund research teams in the participating territories. They do not fund an EU-based observatory directly, and the 2019 theme (marine science) suggests that digital topics are not a given.
- **Remit mismatch.** Forum Macao and IPIM are trade bodies; a policy observatory could be seen as outside scope. Responses may be slow or absent.
- **Neutrality.** Contact with government-linked bodies should keep to published, public-interest material, consistent with the Observatory's neutral, evidence-based stance. Names of territories and bodies follow their own official usage; the Observatory takes no position on status questions.
- **Data.** Only institutional contacts are recorded. Personal names appearing on source pages (e.g. office holders) were deliberately not copied.
- **Region tags.** Hengqin is in Guangdong and is tagged `GBA`; the EU office is seated in Hong Kong and tagged `HK` although its mandate includes Macao.
- **Not found.** No Macao open-source community, OSPO or developer group was located with an official page; no Macao-specific Portuguese chamber of commerce was confirmed (a Portuguese wine chamber in Macao was mentioned in a Forum news item). Neither is in the map.
