# EU-China Cooperation Map

Generated 2026-11-08 for the Europe-China Digital Commons Observatory. Data: `data/cooperation/eu-cn.json` (218 entries). Not committed to git.

## Executive summary

- **218 organisations and initiatives** mapped: EU-level dialogues and frameworks, all 27 EU member states plus the UK, Norway and Switzerland, chambers, think tanks, joint universities and labs, city/region links and open-source bridges.
- **Evidence quality** (HTTP fetch on 2026-11-08): A (official page opened, title read) 176; B (site answered with bot-wall, TLS error or blank page, or existence confirmed by web search only) 32; C (site unreachable from this machine, background knowledge only) 10. Evidence A confirms the website exists, not every descriptive claim; 124 entries point to a parent or generic site because a dedicated page was not located (field `website_is_parent_site`). Descriptions are mostly from background knowledge and must be re-verified before external use.
- **Where real bridges exist.** The most substantive channels are the EU-China S&T agreement and Joint Steering Committee (16th meeting 28 Nov 2024, first in person since 2018, Joint Roadmap negotiations stalled); the Sino-German Center (DFG-NSFC, 25 years in 2025) and the DIN/DKE-SAC standards commission; the Nordic Centre at Fudan and the Sino-Danish Center; and the Eclipse Oniro / OpenHarmony link, the only open-source bridge identified.
- **Digital and open source is thin.** No dedicated EU-China open-source or digital-commons body was found at EU level; the High-Level Digital Dialogue and ICT dialogue are the nearest, and their current status is unclear. Member-state coverage is mostly trade agencies, chambers, think tanks and Confucius Institutes; science and digital-specific bilateral bodies exist mainly for Germany, France, Italy, the Nordics, the Netherlands, Switzerland and the UK.
- **Politically sensitive channels** (Confucius Institutes, China-CEEC, security-linked Chinese think tanks) should be approached with care.
- **Contacts** are institutional only: contact pages found on the organisation's own site, or the generic institutional email published on the official EURAXESS China page. Where no contact link was auto-detected the homepage is given.

## Gaps and caveats

- **Standards channels added 9 Nov:** Austria (ASI–SAC, 2018), France (AFNOR–SAC, 2024). Searched, not confirmed: UNE, SIS, NEN, UNI bilateral agreements with SAC (no result in EN/ZH search); BSI–SAC 2013 (press page now an index, not verified). FR–CN AI declaration 2024 read in full: **no mention of open source** (negative finding).
- **No digital, standards or open-source specific entry** for: Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, United Kingdom, Norway. Entries there are chambers, trade agencies, funders, think tanks or Confucius Institutes. "None found" means none identified in this pass, not that none exists.
- **EU level:** no EU-China open-source or digital-commons forum identified. Status of the High-Level Digital Dialogue (unknown), ICT dialogue (dormant) and Cyber Taskforce (unknown) needs checking against EEAS and DG CNECT sources.
- **Searched but not confirmed (not included):** "ECRAN" (EU-China research and advisory network) and an "EU-China Policy Hub": no web results confirming either. A Dutch chamber of commerce in mainland China: no official site found (search returned the Danish chamber under the same acronym DCCC).
- **Lithuania** left China-CEEC in 2021 and bilateral relations are strained; entries are generic agencies and funders.
- **BICCS (VUB)** is recorded as ended (2009-2017) as a historical precedent.
- **Confucius Institutes:** several have closed or been rebranded in Europe; entries with unknown status should be checked before contact.
- **Chinese sites** (CASS-IES, CICIR and a few European ones) timed out from this machine; see `data/cn_vpn_needed.txt` for the project's VPN queue.
- **Overlap with `data/stakeholders.json`:** entries carry `see_stakeholder` where the organisation or its parent exists there (e.g. Tsinghua, PKU, Fudan, Eclipse, OW2, RISC-V International, Linux Foundation Europe, Committee of the Regions). Deliberately not repeated: EU Delegation to China (`eu-del-china`), Mission of China to the EU (`cn-mission-eu`), EU SME Centre (`dp-eusmec`), EU Chamber of Commerce in China (`dp-euccc`), China Chamber of Commerce to the EU (`dp-ccceu`), MERICS (`ac-merics`), Bruegel (`ac-bruegel`), ECFR (`ac-ecfr`), OpenAtom (`cm-openatom`) and the Chinese embassies listed there.
- **Not yet covered:** sub-national twinnings beyond four German examples; individual Sino-foreign joint labs at CAS institutes; ministry-level S&T agreements per member state; national EURAXESS sites; Hong Kong and Macau institutions beyond Forum Macao.
- **Method:** each website was fetched with a bot-identifying User-Agent; contact links were auto-detected from homepage HTML and not individually opened.
