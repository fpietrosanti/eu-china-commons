**Notes for the ZH reviewer:**
- **自主可控** is used on purpose, because it is the term Chinese policy itself uses.
- **Avoid 共享主权 ("shared sovereignty").** In Chinese political language sovereignty cannot be shared.
- **Avoid 合作共赢 and Belt-and-Road vocabulary**, which are the official diplomatic register (brand rule).
- **Fork:** 分叉 is used here; the alternative to check is 复刻.
