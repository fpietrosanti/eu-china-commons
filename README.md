# eu-china-commons
Digital Commons between Europe and China

**Europe–China Digital Commons Observatory · 中欧数字公地观察站** — an independent research observatory on European and Chinese open-source and digital-commons policy, looking for non-competitive cooperation on the global commons both depend on.

> Work in progress (research phase). Not affiliated with the Digital Commons EDIC, the European Commission, or any government or foundation mentioned here.

## Where to start

Every claim is labelled **fact**, **commitment** (announced target or budget, not a result), **inference** or **proposal**.
