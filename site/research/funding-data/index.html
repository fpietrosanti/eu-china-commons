    Open-source research, not official statistics. These figures are collected from publicly available sources (budgets, programme pages, official filings, press) by the Europe–China Digital Commons Observatory. They are incomplete, may contain errors, and compare instruments that are not always equivalent. Absence of a jurisdiction means we found nothing, not that it spends nothing.

    Help us improve them: open an issue on GitHub (https://github.com/fpietrosanti/eu-china-commons/issues/new?labels=funding-data&title=Funding%20data%3A%20) or write to contact@eu-china-commons.eu. Please include the source (link, document, page) for any correction or addition.

    Method

    • Amounts kept in the original currency and converted to EUR at the ECB annual average rate of the record's year (ECB SDW series EXR/A.CNY.EUR.SP00.A (annual average, CNY per 1 EUR)). Rates used: 2020: 7.8747, 2021: 7.6282, 2022: 7.0788, 2023: 7.66, 2024: 7.7875, 2025: 8.1185 CNY/EUR.
    • Totals include only summable records: disbursed or budgeted, annual, open-source-specific. Multi-year envelopes are shown separately and annualised for reference only. Per-project caps are never summed.
    • Evidence levels A–D as in docs/METHODOLOGY.md §4. Fact = disbursed/budgeted with a source; commitment = envelope or announcement.
