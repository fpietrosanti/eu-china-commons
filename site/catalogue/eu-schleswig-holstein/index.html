Schleswig-Holstein migration to LibreOffice and open source

EuropePublic-sector reuse evidence A verified

State administration moving from Microsoft Office to LibreOffice, then planned Open-Xchange/Thunderbird, Nextcloud and Linux. Trade press reports almost 80% of workstations on LibreOffice by end 2025 and about 20% still needing Microsoft licences; savings claims (over EUR 15M) are the state's own. Linux timetable was unannounced.

since
2024-04 cabinet decision
scale
~80% of workplaces (excl. tax administration) on LibreOffice, Dec 2025; ~44,000 mailboxes on Open-Xchange/Thunderbird completed 2 Oct 2025; >EUR15M licence savings vs EUR9M one-off 2026 investment (state own figures)
