openDesk (ZenDiS, Germany)

EuropePublic-sector reuse evidence A verified

Open-source office and collaboration suite built from European components and operated/maintained by ZenDiS, a federally owned GmbH founded December 2022. Version 1.0 released 20 October 2024; a 2026 press item reports about 100,000 administrative workstations on openDesk and a September 2026 partner programme for private providers. Round 3: opendesk.eu states ZenDiS (founded Dec 2022) operates it for public administration, commissioned by the federal digital ministry (BMDS); the BMDS page sets the target of a sovereign alternative to proprietary workplaces for federal administration by October 2028, piloted in first federal agencies. Workstation counts remain unconfirmed by primary sources.
