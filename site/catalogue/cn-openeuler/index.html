openEuler (OpenAtom)

ChinaIndustrial stack evidence A verified

Server/cloud/embedded OS incubated by the OpenAtom Foundation, MulanPSL2; success stories grouped under finance, carrier, energy, logistics. Claims: cumulative installed base above 16 million by end-2025 (Nov 2025 summit projection); over 10 million at the 2024 summit; 6.31 million community users on 28 Feb 2026 (community bulletin).

since
2019 (open-sourced); OpenAtom since 2021
scale
claim: >16 m installs (2025 projection); 6.31 m users (self-reported)
note
Governance: foundation (OpenAtom), Huawei the dominant sponsor (not read on an official page here). Installed-base figures are community/Huawei claims, unverified; unclear whether cumulative installs or active. Overseas contributors: not stated on the page. Public funding: not documented.
