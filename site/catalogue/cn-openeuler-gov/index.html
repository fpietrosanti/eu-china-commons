openEuler / openKylin in government and public sector

ChinaPublic-sector reuse evidence ? partly verified

Jamestown reports openEuler running State Grid's dispatch-centre system and China Southern Power Grid; Reuters-derived coverage says openKylin (July 2023) was released as China's first homegrown open-source desktop OS, with authorities seeking alternatives for high-security computers. Market-share claims for government (70-90%) come from vendor-promotional Chinese articles and are not accepted. No official .gov.cn adoption data found.
