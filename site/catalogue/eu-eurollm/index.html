EuroLLM (open-weight multilingual model)

EuropeIndustrial stack evidence A verified

Open-weight model family covering 35 languages including the 24 official EU languages (and Chinese), Apache-2.0; developed by a consortium (IST Lisbon, Instituto de Telecomunicacoes, Edinburgh, Unbabel, Aveni, Paris-Saclay, Amsterdam, Naver Labs, Sorbonne, Artefact) with EU funding per the model card.
