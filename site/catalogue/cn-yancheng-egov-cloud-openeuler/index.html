Yancheng (Jiangsu) e-government cloud offers openEuler to municipal projects

ChinaPublic-sector reuse evidence A verified

Correction notice (3) of the Yancheng municipal social-governance command platform phase II procurement, published by the Yancheng Finance Bureau on 4 Mar 2026: OS and DB for domestic adaptation are requested from the data department and provided by the Yancheng e-government cloud; OS options are HuaweiCloudEulerOS, Ubuntu, openEuler. Fact: openEuler is an offered OS of a municipal government cloud used by a named project.
