Eclipse Tractus-X / Catena-X (automotive data space)

EuropeIndustrial stack evidence A verified

Open-source dataspace software (Eclipse Tractus-X) for the Catena-X Automotive Network, whose association is the governance body. Tractus-X is hosted with partners incl. BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Volkswagen, Fraunhofer ISST, IDSA; BMWE listed as partner.
