Catena-X ↔ CAAM/VDA: China–Europe automotive trusted data space

EU–ChinaEU–China bridge evidence A verified

Both sides name each other. CAAM (2025-04-24): CAAM, VDA and Catena-X signed a strategic cooperation agreement at Auto Shanghai. Catena-X: tripartite LoI with CAICT and Sungent (Suzhou, 2025-07-17); China–Europe pilot with 25 Chinese companies (IAA 2025); cross-border data ecosystem with CAAM and VDA China, Zhonglian/众链数智 as operating partner (2026-07-22); CAAM (2026-08-03) reports the ecosystem signing, first use case battery passport. Inference: Catena-X architecture is implemented in the open-source Eclipse Tractus-X; the bridge is on data-space standards and interoperability, open-source code reuse by Chinese side not documented. Government source: Jiangsu Data Bureau (reprinting Suzhou Data Bureau, 2026-05-08) reports the launch of the Catena-X Automotive Network China Digital Clearing House by Sungent, Catena-X, CAICT, Cofinity-X and GIZ.
