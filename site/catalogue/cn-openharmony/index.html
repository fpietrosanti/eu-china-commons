OpenHarmony (OpenAtom) for industry

ChinaIndustrial stack evidence B verified

Open-source OS operated by the OpenAtom Foundation; GitHub mirror (816 repos, sampled ones Apache-2.0) with main development on GitCode. Press reports use in industrial, energy, transport and public-service systems; Eclipse Oniro builds on it.

since
2020 (donated to OpenAtom)
scale
press: >1.6 m community users, 44 SIGs (April 2025)
note
Mirror facts are A; adoption figures come from secondary press and are claims. EU participation: Eclipse Foundation via Oniro (fact, see eu-eclipse-oniro).
