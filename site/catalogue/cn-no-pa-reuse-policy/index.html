No formal PA open-source procurement or reuse policy (as of July 2023)

ChinaGovernance evidence ? verified

The EU-commissioned OFE report states there are no formal procurement or reuse policies specifically addressing OSS in Chinese public administrations, while provinces promote adoption as industrial policy. This is the baseline for the 'harder side' of the PA-reuse comparison and predates the 2025-26 regional plans.

Round 3: superseded in part by municipal plans (Shenzhen 2024, Wuhan 2026, Chongqing 2026) encouraging public-funds use for OpenHarmony; still no national procurement/reuse regime found.
