Wuhan OpenHarmony ecosystem plan with public-funds clause (2026)

ChinaPublic-sector reuse evidence A verified

General Office of Wuhan Municipal Government document Wuzhengban [2026] No.3 (signed 5 Jan, published 15 Jan 2026): 30+ OpenHarmony+government scenarios by 2027 (transport, utility pipelines, tunnels, waterlogging, government services); batch adaptation of municipal government apps with funding guaranteed; Party/government bodies, public institutions and SOEs encouraged to use public funds to build or upgrade OpenHarmony-based IoT systems. Targets, not results.

since
2026-01-15
scale
2027 targets: 100 demo scenarios, 200 apps, 100,000 developers
