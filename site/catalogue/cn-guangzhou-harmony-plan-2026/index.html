Guangzhou Harmony Smart City Action Plan 2026-2028 (government terminals)

ChinaPublic-sector reuse evidence A verified

Guangzhou Municipal Bureau of Industry and IT (Sui Gong Xin Han [2026] No. 192, letter 25 Jun 2026): leverage provincial and municipal open-source HarmonyOS adaptation centres; commitment to convert government terminals to HarmonyOS and embed it in data sharing, administrative approval and regulation; target of at least 100 demonstration scenarios by end-2028. Plan, not outcome. Related reports (secondary): Guangdong OpenHarmony adaptation sub-centre (Guangzhou) unveiled in Baiyun district 21 Mar 2026; provincial service-industry plan (19 Jun 2026, via ITHome) refers to the provincial centre.
