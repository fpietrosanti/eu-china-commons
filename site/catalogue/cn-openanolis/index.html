OpenAnolis / Anolis OS

ChinaIndustrial stack evidence B verified

Linux distribution community founded by Alibaba Cloud with other members in 2020; Anolis OS under Mulan PSL v2 (packages keep own licences); Alibaba reports donation to OpenAtom; chairs/directors largely Alibaba staff; OpenChain ISO/IEC 5230 and 18974 conformance.
