DARE: Digital Autonomy with RISC-V in Europe (EuroHPC)

EuropeIndustrial stack evidence A verified

EuroHPC JU project coordinated by BSC: up to EUR 120 million from EuroHPC JU/Horizon Europe matched by participating states (about EUR 240 m total reported for SGA1); 38 partners from 13 countries; designs one general-purpose RISC-V processor and two accelerators plus a RISC-V HPC/AI software stack. Framework agreement running to 2030.

since
2025-03-06
scale
38 partners, 13 countries
note
The EUR 240 m total is from secondary reporting (heise, Next Platform); the official page gives the EUR 120 m EU share. Open-source licence of outputs not stated. Chinese participants: none documented.
