Sovereign Tech Fund / Agency (Germany)

EuropeFunding evidence ? verified

German programme funding maintenance of open digital infrastructure, run by the Sovereign Tech Agency (subsidiary of SPRIND). The agency's November 2025 newsletter reports EUR 33.6M invested in 96 technologies; a June 2025 Interoperable Europe case study gave over EUR 24.6M for 60+ projects. A heise figure of EUR 114M conflicts and is not used.
