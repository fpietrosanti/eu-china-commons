Gitee as MIIT-endorsed code-hosting platform

ChinaPublic-sector reuse evidence ? verified

MERICS records that in August 2020 MIIT handpicked Gitee as China's independent open-source hosting platform, with reported 5M+ registered members and about 10M projects (platform self-reported, 2021). Not a government code-sharing or reuse platform for administrations as such.

since
2020-08 (MIIT); Gitee 2013
scale
5M+ members, ~10M projects (self-reported, 2021)
