Chinese contributions to the Linux kernel

EU–ChinaExisting collaboration evidence ? partly verified

Huawei ranked first by patches and second by changed lines for Linux 5.10 (Dec 2020) per kernel statistics reported by It's FOSS and The Register; some maintainers criticised low-value commits made to raise metrics. A conference write-up claims Chinese developers contributed over 20% of patches since 2021 (secondary, unverified). Use by European administrations is implicit, not documented here.
