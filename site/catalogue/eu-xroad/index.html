X-Road (NIIS: Estonia, Finland, Iceland)

EuropePublic-sector reuse evidence ? verified

Open-source data-exchange layer for government services. NIIS, a non-profit run by member governments, was founded by Estonia and Finland in 2017; Iceland joined 1 June 2021. Faroe Islands, Aland and Ukraine are partners. An undated Interoperable Europe fact sheet also lists Germany and El Salvador as users.

since
NIIS 2017; Iceland 2021-06-01
scale
3 member states + partners
