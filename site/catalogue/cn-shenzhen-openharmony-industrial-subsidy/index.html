Shenzhen subsidises industrial adoption of OpenHarmony and OpenHarmony-adapted RISC-V chips

ChinaIndustrial stack evidence A verified

Shenzhen Municipal Government General Office, measures to support equipment renewal and technical upgrading of industrial firms (Gazette 2026 no. 9, total 1408), item 5 "support firms using OpenHarmony/RISC-V": upgrade projects of 5 million yuan or more that adopt the OpenHarmony OS get up to 15% of approved investment, plus 5% for devices using RISC-V chips adapted to OpenHarmony in control/drive components; cap 10 million yuan per project; responsible unit Shenzhen MIIT bureau. Fact: first documented public funding instrument for a Chinese open-source industrial stack in our catalogue. Commitment (budget line), not disbursement.
