Decidim (Barcelona)

EuropePublic-sector reuse evidence A verified

Citizen-participation platform developed by Barcelona City Council as free/open-source software, for consultations, debates and participatory budgeting. Start year (reportedly 2016) and the number of institutions using it could not be confirmed from the sources found.

since
2016-02 (Barcelona City Council + community)
scale
>400 instances in 20 countries (City Council page, undated); Generalitat de Catalunya adopted; 2021 tripartite agreement; development agreement approved 2025-11-26
