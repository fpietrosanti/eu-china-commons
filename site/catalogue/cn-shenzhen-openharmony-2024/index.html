Shenzhen 2024 action plan for OpenHarmony native apps

ChinaPublic-sector reuse evidence A verified

Issued by the Shenzhen Bureau of Industry and Information Technology (hosted on sz.gov.cn, 3 Mar 2024; a joint issue with the Government Services and Data Bureau appears in a search listing): publicly funded government-service software should launch OpenHarmony native versions in 2024; public bodies asked to build city-management and public-service apps; healthcare/education verticals targeted. Targets, no outcome data.

since
2024-03-03
scale
targets: >1,000 qualified firms, ~500,000 developers, no budget stated
