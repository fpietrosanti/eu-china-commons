BwMessenger (Bundeswehr, Matrix/Element)

EuropePublic-sector reuse evidence A verified

Matrix-based secure messenger for the German armed forces, announced with Element in November 2020 for more than 50,000 users. Later vendor material reports more than 100,000 active users. No independent Bundeswehr/BWI count found.

since
2020-11 (BWI first-anniversary article, Nov 2021)
scale
~65,000 downloads in first year (BWI); >100,000 users later claim (press 2023)
